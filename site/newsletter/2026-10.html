The Deadline – issue 1, October 2026

MTD update 2 is due 7 November

In one line: if your qualifying income is over £50,000, your records to 5 October must reach HMRC through recognised software by 7 November.

1. MTD: your second quarterly update

  • It's cumulative: send totals from 6 April to 5 October (or 1 April to 30 September if you chose calendar quarters).
  • A mistake in your first update? This update corrects it automatically.
  • Late updates earn penalty points, and a fine applies once you reach the threshold. See gov.uk for details.

2. Landlords: five months into the Renters' Rights Act

  • Section 21 ended on 1 May 2026, so possession now needs a Section 8 ground.
  • The information-sheet deadline for existing tenancies (31 May) has passed. If you missed it, send it now: fines go up to £7,000.
  • Rent increases are once a year, by Section 13 notice only.

3. Selling to the EU? Accessibility applies

The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many businesses selling online to EU consumers. Free tools such as WAVE or Lighthouse are a good first check.

4. Dates for your diary

  • 31 Oct 2026: paper Self Assessment return deadline (2025-26)
  • 7 Nov 2026: MTD quarterly update 2
  • 31 Jan 2027: online Self Assessment return and payment
