HMRC mileage allowance calculator

Work out the tax-free mileage allowance for business journeys in one tax year.

Rates: cars and vans 45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year, then 25p; motorcycles 24p; bicycles 20p; 5p per passenger per mile. Self-employed people who use these rates can't also claim the vehicle's actual running costs. Checked against gov.uk on 25 September 2026.

Keeping records? The MTD Ready workbook has a mileage log that works this out as you go.
