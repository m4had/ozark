Late-payment interest calculator (UK business invoices)

For invoices to other businesses. The Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act lets you claim interest and fixed compensation.

Reference rate is the Bank of England Bank Rate on 31 December (for debts due January–June) or 30 June (July–December): 3.75% for 2026. Compensation: £40 (under £1,000), £70 (£1,000–£9,999.99), £100 (£10,000+). Only for business-to-business debts; your contract may set its own terms. Checked 25 September 2026. Not legal advice.
