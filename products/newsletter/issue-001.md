# The Deadline – issue 1 (October 2026)
*UK small-business rules in plain English, once a month. Free.*

**This month in one line:** the next Making Tax Digital quarterly update is due **7 November**. If your qualifying income is over £50,000, your records to 5 October need to be in HMRC-recognised software by then.

### 1. MTD: your second quarterly update
- It's cumulative: send totals from 6 April to 5 October (or 1 April–30 September if you chose calendar quarters).
- A mistake in your first update? This update corrects it automatically.
- Missed a deadline? MTD uses a points-based penalty system: each missed update earns a point, and a fine applies once you reach the threshold. Check gov.uk for the details.
→ Source: gov.uk, "Making Tax Digital for Income Tax"

### 2. Landlords: 5 months into the Renters' Rights Act
- Section 21 ended on 1 May 2026, so possession now needs a Section 8 ground.
- The information-sheet deadline for existing tenancies (31 May) has passed. If you missed it, send it now: fines go up to £7,000.
- Rent increases are once a year, by Section 13 notice only.
→ Source: legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/421

### 3. Selling to the EU? Accessibility applies
The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025 to many businesses selling online to EU consumers. A quick first step is an automated scan of your site. Free tools such as WAVE or Lighthouse catch common problems.
