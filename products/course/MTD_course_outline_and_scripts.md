## Lesson 1 – Does MTD apply to me? (6 min)
- **Qualifying income:** gross self-employment plus property income, before expenses, from your 2024-25 return. Over £50k → MTD from 6 Apr 2026. Over £30k → from 6 Apr 2027. Over £20k → from 6 Apr 2028.
- **Exemptions:** digitally excluded people can apply. Partnership income isn't in scope yet.
- **On screen:** gov.uk "Check if you're eligible" tool.
- **Script opener:** "If you earn over fifty thousand pounds from self-employment or property, the way you report to HMRC changed this April. In six minutes you'll know whether it applies to you and when."

## Lesson 2 – What MTD actually asks you to do (8 min)
- Keep digital records of each income and expense transaction.
- Send a quarterly update to HMRC. It's cumulative, due 7 Aug, 7 Nov, 7 Feb and 7 May.
- File a final declaration by 31 January after the tax year ends.
- **Myth-busting:** you don't pay tax quarterly. Payment dates are unchanged.

## Lesson 3 – Choosing software: all-in-one vs spreadsheet + bridging (8 min)
- HMRC's list of recognised software.
- The trade-off: an all-in-one app has a monthly fee but is simple. A spreadsheet plus bridging software is cheaper and familiar.
- **Demo:** where the workbook's year-to-date totals go in bridging software.

## Lesson 4 – Setting up your records (10 min)
- Workbook walkthrough: Settings, standard vs calendar quarters, the income and expense sheets.
- Categories explained with examples, including splitting private use.
- Simplified expenses: mileage at 45p/25p and use of home. You can't mix mileage with actual vehicle costs for the same vehicle.

## Lesson 5 – Your first quarterly update, step by step (8 min)
- Check totals, reconcile to your bank, copy the year-to-date figures, submit, and keep the confirmation.
- Fixing mistakes: the next cumulative update corrects them.

## Lesson 6 – A 20-minute monthly routine (5 min)
- Bank feed or statement → workbook → receipts filed → mileage log. Use the deadline countdown.
- Where to get help: HMRC helpline, gov.uk guidance, finding an accountant (check they're AML-supervised).
